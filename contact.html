Common Questions

Frequently Asked

We're a small care home. Can we actually afford your services?

Absolutely. Our GRC-as-a-Subscription service starts at $1,500/month for small facilities — designed specifically for organizations that need compliance support without enterprise budgets. We also offer a free initial assessment so there's no risk in starting the conversation.

How is Comforis different from a general cybersecurity firm?

We serve healthcare exclusively. We understand clinical workflows, the difference between an IT outage and a clinical crisis, and the specific regulatory obligations under PHIPA, PIPA, and Bill C-26. Generic firms repurpose corporate frameworks. We build for healthcare from the ground up.

Do you work with US healthcare organizations?

Yes. We support cross-border compliance for Canadian telehealth platforms operating in the US, and can assist US healthcare organizations through HIPAA alignment. Our primary market is Canada, but our frameworks are built with global applicability in mind.

How quickly can you get started?

For urgent situations — an upcoming audit, a recent incident, or a legislative deadline — we can mobilize within days. Standard engagements typically begin within two to three weeks of contract signing.

What does the free assessment include?

A 30-60 minute structured conversation with one of our specialists, followed by a short written brief identifying your highest-risk gaps relative to your regulatory obligations and current controls. No obligation to engage further — we think transparency builds better relationships than pressure.
