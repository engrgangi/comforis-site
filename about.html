Our Mission

Governance That Protects the People Who Protect Patients

When a hospital gets hit by ransomware, it's not just a data breach — it's patients diverted, surgeries cancelled, lives disrupted. Cybersecurity in healthcare has consequences that don't exist in any other sector.

We founded Comforis because the GRC market had a blind spot: hospitals, care homes, and health networks were being served by generic cybersecurity firms who treated them like any other enterprise client. They got repurposed corporate frameworks, unfamiliar with clinical workflows, regulatory nuance, or what it means when a nurse can't access a patient record at 3am.

Our approach is different. We build governance frameworks that account for clinical realities. We write incident response playbooks that include the ICU. We train staff in a way that works for shift workers, not 9-to-5 desk employees. And we speak the language of healthcare boards — risk in plain language, not technical jargon.

✓

Healthcare-First Thinking — Every framework, policy, and assessment is designed around clinical environments, not adapted from elsewhere.

✓

Proof, Not Intent — Regulators and insurers demand evidence. We build audit-ready documentation, not just plans.

✓

Accessible to All Tiers — From large hospital networks to 40-bed care homes, we build services that fit every budget.

✓

Standing Partnership — We're not a one-time audit. We're a long-term governance partner that evolves as the threat landscape does.
